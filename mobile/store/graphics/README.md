# Store graphics

| File | Size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `icon-512.png` | 512 × 512 | Play Console app icon (from `mobile/assets/icon.png`) |
| `feature-graphic-bn.png`, `feature-graphic-en.png` | 1024 × 500 | Feature graphic for the Bangla and English listing |
| `screenshots/bn/*.png`, `screenshots/en/*.png` | 1080 × 1920 | Phone screenshots, in this order |

The screenshots are the real app (its web build, phone size) with a demo farm
("Rahim Dairy & Poultry": 6 cows, a heifer, a fattening bull, a layer flock
of 1,200, 30 days of milk and eggs, vaccines, sales and expenses), framed with
a caption. When the app's screens change a lot, ask for them to be remade.
