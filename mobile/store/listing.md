# Google Play store listing

Copy these into Play Console → *Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing*.
Add English as the default language and Bangla (bn-BD) as a translation.
Limits: app name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4,000.

---

## English (en-US) — default

**App name** (23/30)

```
FarmFlows: Farm Manager
```

**Short description** (77/80)

```
Dairy, cattle, goat & poultry records: milk, eggs, vaccines, profit. Offline.
```

**Full description**

```
FarmFlows keeps your whole farm in one simple app — in Bangla or English, and it works without internet.

Made for dairy farms, cattle fattening and Qurbani, goats and sheep, layer flocks and broiler or Sonali batches.

DAILY ENTRY IN A FEW TAPS
• Milk for every cow, morning and evening, with “Same as yesterday”
• Eggs, deaths and feed for each flock
• Sales, buyers' dues and expenses
• No internet? The app still opens with all your farm's data; entries wait on the phone and are sent by themselves later

ANIMAL RECORDS
• Every cow, goat and sheep with photo, tag, breed and parents
• Scan the QR ear tag to open an animal
• Weight by scale or measuring tape, daily gain and fattening progress

HEALTH AND BREEDING
• Vaccines, deworming and treatments, for one animal or many at once
• Reminders before a vaccine or calving is due
• Heat, AI, pregnancy check, calving and dry-off
• Your vet can add records for you to approve

MONEY
• Income and expenses, cost per litre of milk and per egg
• Profit for each animal and for the month
• Feed and medicine stock with low-stock alerts
• Staff, attendance and salary

FREE FARM TOOLS
• Cattle and goat weight from a measuring tape (in kg)
• Feed ration and home-made concentrate mix with cost
• Poultry feed and water, calving date, Qurbani price, medicine dose, unit converter (maund, seer, decimal, bigha) and more

TEAM AND REPORTS
• Add family members, workers and your vet, each with the right access
• Milk and egg charts, reports to share as PDF or CSV
• The same account works on farmflows.app on a computer

Start free. Paid plans for larger farms are available on the website.
```
